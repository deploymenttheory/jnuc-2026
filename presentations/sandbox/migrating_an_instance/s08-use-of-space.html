Slide 11 - Migration wave workflow

The feedback was that the content is already right and only the use of the space needs work: today the seven steps sit in one narrow row across the top third and roughly four hundred pixels of the slide are empty. Nothing here changes a word, an element or the order - all four options carry the same seven steps, the same lock on step 3 and the same title, and differ only in how they are laid out and how big the type gets. Each render below is the live slide with that option's CSS layered on top.

ABCD

Option A - The single row live now

The deck as it ships today: seven equal cards in one row, body text at caption size, and the bottom 40 per cent of the slide empty. Pick this if the row of seven reads well enough from the back of the room and the empty half is worth keeping as breathing space.

Option B - Two rows, four then three

    
The seven cards wrap onto a second row, four over three, so each card takes back the height the single row was leaving empty. The body text moves up from caption size to slide size and the numbers to title size, and small chevrons sit in the gaps to carry the direction of travel along each row. Pick this if you want the cards kept as cards and the steps still weighted equally, just readable from further back.

Option C - Seven bands stepping down

    
The row becomes seven full-width bands running down the slide, each one starting a notch further in than the band above it and all of them ending flush right, so the sequence reads as a descent rather than a grid. Every step fits on a single line at slide size, with the numbers in an aligned gutter and a coloured left edge on each band - red on step 3. Pick this if you want the slide read as a checklist the speaker walks down, one line at a time.

Option D - The freeze in the middle

    
Three columns at three different weights. Steps 1 and 2 stack down the left, step 3 becomes a full-height centre panel with the lock at scale and its text at heading size, and steps 4 to 7 run down the right in a compact numbered column. The shape of the slide becomes everything before the freeze, the freeze, everything after. Pick this if step 3 is the point of the slide and you want the layout to say so before the speaker does.
