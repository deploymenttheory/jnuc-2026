Slide 12 - Migration wave workflow, an eighth step

Joseph asked for an eighth step on refinement and making the code DRY. That step is in the deck now, after step 7 ("Announce to all teams: the resource is now Terraform-managed"): "Refine the generated HCL into a DRY for_each map: dedupe, name the IDs, share the locals," grounded in slide 14's refinement passes and the settled fact that refinement ran through February and March, after the December-January bulk waves closed. It carries the i-squares-2x2 icon already used for slide 14's "Ours, after the passes" panel and slide 18's "Shared" row, and the .slide-sub line now counts eight steps. The open question is how the eighth band sits: a plain peer of 1 to 7, or set apart as work that happens after a wave closes rather than inside one, and whether the staircase indent - now one notch deeper - is worth keeping. Option B - Set apart, after the wave closes

    
Step 8 is not part of the freeze-and-import sequence the other seven describe - it is the pass that happened once resources were already Terraform-managed, running on its own timeline through February and March rather than inside each wave. This option says so visually: a hairline rule breaks the sequence above it, the band drops the staircase indent to sit flush left rather than travelling one more notch, and its left edge and number turn the deck's muted grey instead of the accent lime, reading as a different kind of step rather than a numbered continuation. The wording also shifts to name the moment: "Once the wave closes, refine the generated HCL into a DRY for_each map: dedupe, name the IDs, share the locals." The equivalent deck edit is the same CSS on #s08 .stepper li:nth-child(8) plus that sentence in the markup. Pick this if the slide should make the timing distinction itself, rather than leaving it to the speaker notes.

Option C - Square the staircase off

    
Rather than let the descent travel an eighth notch, every band drops its indent and lines up flush left - the staircase motif is retired for this slide rather than stretched to fit one more row. The vertical padding stays tightened to --sp-1, the same as option A: dropping the indent frees width, not height, so it does not undo the reason that tightening was needed. What it does buy is a slide that no longer keeps travelling rightward as it grows, and a sequence that reads as a plain numbered checklist rather than a descent - which also means step 8 no longer stands out from the seven before it. Pick this if the diagonal cue was decorative rather than load-bearing, and a uniform list reads just as well at eight steps as it did at seven.
