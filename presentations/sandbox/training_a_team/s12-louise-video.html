Slide 12 - Louise's journey, with the interview embedded

Dafydd: embed presentations/_shared/louise_story_final.mp4 into the slide, and reorganise the layout first because the video's frame does not fit the space the placeholder holds. The file is 1280x720, 16:9, 2 minutes 52 seconds, H.264 and AAC, about 40 MB. The placeholder it replaces is a 798 by 760 box, so a 16:9 picture dropped straight into it would be 798 by 449 with 311px of dead space underneath: that mismatch is what every option below is solving.

Identical in all four: the eyebrow, the title, the quote and all four journey lines are the words already on the slide, unchanged. Each option is the real deck slide with its contents replaced and the option's CSS added, and each carries the real video, so what you see below is the actual file playing in the actual slide. Press play in any of them. All four are scoped to a .louise class, which the deck edit adds to the section, so an accepted option pastes in as one block of rules.

One thing to decide alongside the layout. The deck advances a slide on the space bar wherever focus is, so with a real <video controls> on the slide, pressing space to play would both start the video and jump to slide 13. Whichever option is accepted, the key handler needs to ignore keys aimed at the video. That is a one-line change and it ships with the option unless you say otherwise.

And one more. A <video> with no poster shows a black rectangle until it is played, which is what the renders below are doing. The deck can either keep that, or carry a still from the file as the poster frame so the slide looks finished before you press play. A poster is one extra image in _shared/. Say which you want and it ships with the option.

Option A - Video leads, journey alongside

Closest to what the slide does today, with the proportions fixed. The video takes a 1000px column at its true 16:9 shape (562px tall, so it stops being a 760px box holding a 449px picture) and the pull quote moves underneath it, where it reads as the caption to what you have just watched. The four journey lines stack down a 604px column beside it at slightly smaller type. Nothing is reordered and nothing is cut, so this is the safe pick if the slide is right and only the video was wrong.

Option D - Native frame
