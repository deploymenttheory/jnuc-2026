Proposed speaker notes

Explain the seven approaches that helped the team learn. Keep the practical detail with each point, and take time over the conditions that made secondments useful.

Paired programming transfers knowledge in real time. Engineers learn faster from a peer than from documentation alone. Effective pairs work with discipline: they stay on one topic at a time, clarify understanding in stages and deliberately share the context behind the work. They go beyond simply answering the questions the learner happens to ask.

We scaled training back to foundational principles the team did not already know, starting with Git. We taught each discipline individually and built understanding incrementally before introducing the full picture. Those foundations gave engineers something to build on in their later self-directed learning.

Grouping engineers into learning cohorts with similar skill levels helped us keep the pace right. The aim was to avoid leaving some people bored while others fell behind.

Engineers need dedicated non-production sandboxes where they can experiment freely without fear of breaking production. Keep the experiments in those safe lab environments.

Secondments and team shadowing provide grounding and broad exposure, even when the host team uses other Terraform providers. This is a specific approach, and it only works when a platform engineering team has time to dedicate to training the seconded engineer. Where that time is unavailable, lean on an enthusiastic employee who is willing to learn independently. Prior experience with GitHub and scripting or software development helps the engineer going on secondment.

The Terraform registry and other teams' open-source reference implementations worked as learning material. They provide known good working examples of individual resources and inspiration for a syllabus. Our own documentation only worked after we cut it back to the essentials. The replacement curriculum contained 3,778 words across two modules and four labs, with one lab deliberately broken. It was 57 times smaller than the 214,645-word manual it replaced. Both versions are public in the training repo for people to compare.

Close with patience. Engineers have a broad range of concepts to cover, and developing that understanding takes time.
